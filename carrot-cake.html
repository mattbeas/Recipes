Chapter 5.1: Cakes, Cupcakes, and Tortes

Carrot Cake

  • IBS
  • GF

Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups + 2 tbsp gluten-free flour (Authentic Foods Classic Blend works great)
  • 1 tsp xanthan gum (omit if the flour already contains xanthan gum or psyllium husk)
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1¼ cups cooking oil (peanut oil is nutty; olive oil also works well)
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 4 eggs, slightly beaten (can substitute 6 egg whites)
  • 1 (8¼ oz) can crushed pineapple, drained
  • 1 cup walnuts, chopped
  • 2 cups grated carrots (about 3 large carrots)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Lightly grease or spray a 13×9-inch cake pan.
  3. Sift together the flour, xanthan gum, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, and nutmeg, then set aside.
  4. In a large bowl, mix together the sugar, oil, vanilla, eggs, and pineapple.
  5. Combine the dry ingredients with the sugar and oil mixture, and mix until combined.
  6. Add the chopped walnuts and grated carrots, and mix on low speed until combined.
  7. Transfer the batter to the prepared pan. Bake for 45 minutes, until golden brown and a toothpick comes out clean.
  8. Let the cake cool before frosting.

Frosting

Ingredients

  • ⅓ cup butter, room temperature
  • 5 to 6 oz lactose-free cream cheese, softened
  • 1½ cups powdered sugar (sift after measuring)
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Steps

  1. Combine all the ingredients until smooth. Frost the cake once it has cooled.

Variations

Not IBS-safe version: If using wheat flour, omit the extra 2 tbsp of flour and the xanthan gum. Regular cream cheese can be used.
